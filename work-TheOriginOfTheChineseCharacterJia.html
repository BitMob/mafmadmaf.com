        創作筆記
（一）豭（讀音 jiā），指公豬，亦指代雄性動物。甲骨文「家」字的象形為在居室之下養豬（豕，讀音 shǐ）。但「家」字的讀音卻是跟隨「豭」字的省聲（省聲，指漢字形聲字的聲符為某字的省略）。豭，甲骨文像公豬之形，並畫出生殖器。小篆變為從「豕」，聲「叚」，本義為公豬，之後才泛指豬。
（二）我問正在廚房熱火朝天地操刀年夜飯的二叔和他的大哥（我的父親）：我們平常吃的豬肉是公豬肉還是母豬肉？他們說，公豬的肉好吃很多。因為母豬的肉質和營養都隨著過長的生長時間而逐漸下降，體內的不良元素卻逐漸積累。
（三）創造出「家」字的遠古之人，我猜是雌性動物。
實地聲音記錄於 2021 年 2 月的中國農歷新年除夕（年三十）團年飯。原始的線性記錄是從廚房備菜、烹調一直到餐桌上的家人聚餐，場景分為「廚房」和「餐桌」前後兩個部分。在《家字的由來》作品中，則將「廚房」和「餐桌」分別置於立體聲的左和右聲道，同時播放；並且即興或非即興地填充合成聲響。
mafmadmaf, 2023.03.29

        Artist's Note
(1) 豭 (jiā) means a male pig and can also refer to male animals. In oracle-bone script, the character 家 depicts a pig (豕, pronounced shǐ) being raised under a roof. However, 家 takes its pronunciation from the abbreviated phonetic component of 豭. In oracle-bone script, 豭 resembles a male pig, with its reproductive organs depicted. In small seal script, it combines 豕 with the phonetic component 叚. Its original meaning was a male pig, before it came to refer to pigs in general.
(2) As my second uncle and his elder brother (my father) were busy preparing the Chinese New Year's Eve dinner in the kitchen, I asked them: “Is the pork we usually eat from male pigs or female pigs?” They said that meat from male pigs tastes much better, because the quality and nutritional value of meat from female pigs gradually decline as they grow older, while undesirable substances accumulate in their bodies.
(3) I suspect that the ancient person who created the character 家 was a female animal.
The field recording was made during a family reunion dinner on Chinese New Year's Eve in February 2021. The original linear recording follows the preparation and cooking in the kitchen through to the family gathering at the dinner table, with the “kitchen” and “dinner table” forming two successive parts. In The Origin of the Chinese Character '家', the “kitchen” and “dinner table” are placed in the left and right stereo channels respectively and played simultaneously, with improvised and non-improvised synthesized sounds added.
mafmadmaf, 2023.03.29
